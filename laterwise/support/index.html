Frequently asked questions

Saving

How do I save something to Laterwise?

iPhone and iPad: tap Share in any app (Safari, Photos, Files, Mail, YouTube…) and choose Laterwise. If you don't see it, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and add Laterwise to your favorites.

Mac: press ⌥⌘S from any app to save the clipboard or a screenshot, or drop a link, file or image on the Quick Capture panel. You can also select text or files and choose Services › Save to Laterwise, or use Share.

What is "why did you save this" for?

When you save, you can tap a reason: read, buy, try, go, decide, reference, or for someone. Laterwise uses it to sort the save and to decide when to bring it back. If you skip it, Laterwise suggests one, and you can change it any time.

How do I turn on Laterwise in Safari?

Safari adds Laterwise's extension turned off. Turn it on once, and allow it on every website: text you select in Safari is then saved with its page, and your highlights show again on their pages.

iPhone and iPad: in Laterwise, open Settings, and under Highlights from Safari tap Turn On… next to Laterwise in Safari (from iOS 26.2 it opens Safari's settings at Laterwise). Or open the Settings app › Apps › Safari › Extensions › Laterwise, turn it on, and allow it on all websites.

Mac: in Laterwise, open Settings › Capture › Laterwise in Safari › Turn On…, or in Safari choose Safari › Settings › Extensions and select Laterwise. Then click Edit Websites and set "When visiting other websites" to Allow.

The welcome screens show these steps the first time you open Laterwise; Settings › About › Show Welcome Again shows them again.

I shared text from Safari, but it was saved without its page.

Safari shares selected text without its link. Laterwise's Safari extension notes the page you selected it on, so turn it on and allow it on every website (see above). Until then, the Save sheet says so under the text.

How do highlights work?

Select text on a page in Safari and share it to Laterwise. Every highlight of the same page joins one item, in the order you saved them, each with its own note. Open at Highlight opens the page in Safari scrolled to that passage, with the page's other highlights marked too.

Free keeps up to 3 highlights per page together; a 4th is still saved, as an item of its own. With Pro, any number stay together, in five colors, Copy All copies them as Markdown, and Safari draws them on their page again when you come back. With Pro, selecting text in Safari also shows a bar of colors: tap one to save it as a highlight in that color.

Shopping and prices

How does price comparison work?

Save the same product from several shops (Safari, or a shop's app via Share). Laterwise reads each page's price and groups the same product together, cheapest first. Comparing the shops side by side and Auto Price Watch, which checks prices for you and tells you when one drops, are part of Pro.

A product shows "Sign In" or "No Price".

Some shops show a price only to people signed in, or only after you pick a size. Open the item and tap Open Shop Page: sign in, or pick the size, on the shop's own page inside Laterwise, and the price it shows is read. Laterwise remembers the sign-in for that shop. You can also pick the price on the page, or type it.

It says "This Shop Doesn't Let Apps In".

A few shops turn away pages opened by apps. Tap Open in Safari, then share the page from Safari to Laterwise: the price is read from the page you see there.

Tickets, PDFs and scans

How do I get reminded before a flight or a show?

Save the ticket or itinerary (a PDF, a page, a Wallet pass or a screenshot). Laterwise reads the dates and destination and reminds you a day before and a few hours before; change the times on the item. Once the trip has passed, it moves to Done on its own.

A PDF says "Sign In to Get the PDF".

The site gives that PDF only to people signed in, or with a code (an airline ticket's link, a journal article). Open the item, tap Sign In to Get the PDF, and sign in or enter the code on the site's page inside Laterwise. The PDF is kept with the item and read as soon as the site sends it.

Can I scan price tags and book pages?

On iPhone and iPad, tap + in Later. Scan Price Tag reads the product and price from a tag in a store, and can suggest the shop you're in from the shops nearby (it asks for your location only then, and only while the app is open). Save Book Pages scans pages into a searchable PDF and finds the book by its barcode; scanning a whole book into one PDF is part of Pro.

Screenshot Rescue

What does Screenshot Rescue do?

On iPhone and iPad, it brings the screenshots in your Photos library into Laterwise, reads the text in them, and sorts them into receipts, tickets, events, recipes, quotes, products and more. Open Screenshots in the sidebar to start, or accept the offer when you first open the app.

Will it delete my photos?

Only if you ask it to. Clean Up Photos deletes the screenshots that are already in Laterwise, and iOS asks you to confirm first. Deleted screenshots stay in Photos › Recently Deleted for 30 days, and they stay in Laterwise, searchable by their text.

It's taking a while. Can I leave the app?

Yes. The import keeps going in the background, with progress shown by the system. Sorting a large library on the device can take a while longer, and it carries on whenever Laterwise is open.

Laterwise says it can't see my photos.

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Laterwise and choose Full Access, or Limited Access with the screenshots you want to include.

Sorting and Apple Intelligence

Which devices sort and summarize my saves?

Titles, summaries and dates come from Apple Intelligence on your device. On a device without Apple Intelligence, Laterwise still saves, reads text and searches, with simpler titles. When iCloud sync is on, a device that has Apple Intelligence improves those saves, and the results appear on your other devices too.

Is my content sent to a server for AI?

No. The AI runs on your device. See the privacy policy.

iCloud sync

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on each device. Sync is on by default; check Laterwise › Settings › iCloud. Sync Now fetches and sends right away.

My saves aren't showing up on another device.

Check that iCloud is on for the device and has free storage, that both devices use the same Apple Account, and that Settings › iCloud in Laterwise doesn't show a message. Then tap Sync Now on both devices. Large files can take longer than the rest.

Can I save iCloud space?

Turn off Sync Original Files in Settings. Titles, notes, text, summaries and small previews still sync; the full-size originals stay on the device you saved them on.

Moments and the Weekly Review

What are Moments?

Notifications that bring a save back when it's useful: the evening before an event, the day before a deadline or sale ends, five and one days before a plan's date, and the morning an action is due. You can set how many a day and quiet hours in Settings. Moments are part of Laterwise Pro.

I stopped getting a kind of reminder.

If you dismiss the same kind of reminder three times in a row, Laterwise pauses it for 30 days. Opening or acting on one resets that.

What is the Weekly Review?

A short stack of cards on the day and time you choose: what's coming up, this week's saves, and things you haven't touched in a month. Do it, keep it or toss it. The Weekly Review is free.

Laterwise Pro

What does Pro add?

Price comparison and Auto Price Watch, every highlight on a page in color and shown again in Safari, Moments, Briefs for every group, unlimited smart groups (Free keeps 3), and whole-book scans. Saving, search, your library, sync, Screenshot Rescue, the Weekly Review, reminders before flights and shows, and up to 3 highlights per page are always free. The yearly plan starts with a 14-day free trial. See pricing.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it.

Open Settings › Laterwise Pro and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. One purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone or iPad: Laterwise › Settings › Laterwise Pro › Manage Subscription, or Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. On Mac: App Store › your name › Account Settings › Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the Laterwise purchase.
