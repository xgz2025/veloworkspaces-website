Laterwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: Laterwise has no account and no server of its own. What you save stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud database. The AI that sorts and summarizes it runs on your device. Laterwise collects no usage data, has no analytics or advertising, and doesn't track you.

What Laterwise stores, and where

Everything you save (links, page text, highlights, screenshots, photos, PDFs, files and your notes), and everything Laterwise works out about it (titles, summaries, dates, places, groups, Briefs and actions), is stored on your device, inside the app's own container.

If iCloud sync is on (it is by default, and you can turn it off in Laterwise's Settings), the library is also kept in your private iCloud database, so it can appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The contents of each save are stored in end-to-end encrypted fields that only your devices can read. Attached files, the originals and their small previews, are stored as iCloud assets in the same private database. They're end-to-end encrypted when Advanced Data Protection is on for your Apple Account. You can stop original files from syncing with "Sync Original Files" in Settings. We, the developer, have no access to your iCloud data.

The AI runs on your device

Laterwise uses Apple Intelligence's on-device language model, Apple's on-device text recognition (Vision) and on-device language embeddings to read, sort, summarize and search your saves. This processing happens on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Laterwise doesn't send your content to us or to any AI service.

Network access the app does use

  • The pages you save. To read a saved link's title, text, price and details, Laterwise downloads that page directly from its website, the same way a browser would, and checks a saved product's page again for its price when you ask or, with Auto Price Watch, on the schedule you set. For a paper, it also reads the paper's own page (on arXiv, for instance, or through its DOI). The website sees an ordinary request from your device.
  • Pages you open inside Laterwise. When you open a shop's page or a PDF's site in Laterwise (Open Shop Page, Sign In to Get the PDF), it's a browser inside the app: anything you sign in with or type there goes only to that website. Laterwise keeps that website's cookies on your device so you stay signed in, never sends them anywhere else, and never sees your password. These pages are never given your location.
  • Book lookups. When you scan a book's barcode, its ISBN (only the number) is looked up in public book catalogs (Apple Books, Google Books, Open Library and, for Chinese books, Douban) to find the title and author.
  • Apple Maps, to name the place where a photo you save was taken ("Shibuya, Tokyo"), when the photo itself includes a location, and, when you scan a price tag, to list the shops near you so you can pick the one you're in. Only those coordinates are sent, and only to Apple. Photos without a location, and everything else you save, are never sent.
  • iCloud, for sync, if it's on.
  • The App Store, if you buy Laterwise Pro or restore a purchase.

None of these go through a server we operate.

Permissions the app may ask for

Laterwise asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Photos (iPhone and iPad): when you start Screenshot Rescue, to read the screenshots in your library. Laterwise deletes screenshots from Photos only when you choose Clean Up Photos and confirm in the system's own dialog. Deleted photos stay in Photos' Recently Deleted for 30 days.
  • Notifications: on the welcome screen, under "Back when it matters", or when you first set a reminder. Without it, Laterwise works the same, just without reminders.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad): when you scan a price tag or book pages. Images stay on your device, saved only if you save the scan.
  • Location, while using the app (iPhone and iPad): only when you scan a price tag, to suggest the shop you're in. It isn't kept, and it's never used in the background.
  • Screen Recording (Mac): only when you capture a screenshot with Quick Capture. Laterwise's own windows are left out of the capture.

Files you share to Laterwise are copied into the app. Laterwise has no access to anything else on your device.

The Safari extension

Laterwise's Safari extension works only when you turn it on and allow it on websites. It notes, on your device only, the page you're on and the last text you selected (for half an hour), so that text you share from Safari is saved with its page, and the link of a PDF you open, so a shared PDF keeps its link. With Laterwise Pro, it reads the highlights you saved for the page you're on from Laterwise's files on your device, to draw them there. It doesn't record your browsing, and it sends nothing to us or to any server.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

Laterwise contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to us automatically. We don't build a profile of you, and we don't sell or share data with anyone, because we don't collect any. If you choose to share crash reports or usage data with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide anonymized crash reports to us under Apple's own privacy policy.

Purchases

Laterwise Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment and gives the app only what it needs to unlock Pro. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account.

Deleting your data

  • Delete items or groups in the app. Deletions also apply in iCloud and on your other devices.
  • To remove Laterwise's iCloud data entirely: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Laterwise. On Mac, open System Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage. Sync then turns itself off in Laterwise until you turn it back on.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Children's privacy

Laterwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. The app collects none from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Since there's no account, we can't notify you individually, so check back here to review the current version.
