Features

From saved to done.

Most saved things are never seen again. Nextmark keeps the reason with each save and does the sorting for you, so what you saved turns into something you actually do.

Say why, in one tap

Share a page, a photo, a PDF or a passage to Nextmark and tap why you saved it: to read, buy, try, go, decide, keep for reference, or for someone. Weeks later it opens with the reason you had.

  • Share sheet on iPhone and iPad, Safari, and every app that shares
  • On Mac: press ⌥⌘S anywhere, use Services, or drop files on the Quick Capture panel
  • Highlights from Safari reopen the page at the exact passage

It sorts itself, on your device

Apple Intelligence reads what you saved and gives it a clear title, a summary, its kind (recipe, product, event, ticket, receipt…), and the dates and places in it. Nothing is sent to a server to do this.

  • Text in screenshots and photos is read with Apple's on-device Vision
  • Related saves are proposed as groups you can keep or dismiss
  • Search by meaning, not just words: "that ramen place in Tokyo"

Back when it mattersPro

Moments bring a save back at the right time: the evening before the concert, the day before the sale ends, five days before your trip, on the morning an action is due. At most a few a day, never during your quiet hours.

  • The Weekly Review stays free: do it, keep it or toss it, one card at a time
  • Types you keep dismissing pause themselves for a month
  • Done, Snooze and Open right from the notification

Screenshot Rescue

Thousands of screenshots in Photos on your iPhone or iPad? Nextmark brings them in, reads them, and sorts out the receipts, tickets, recipes, quotes and products, so the useful ones are findable and the rest can go.

  • Runs in the background with the system's progress bar
  • Clean Up Photos deletes the originals only when you confirm
  • New screenshots are picked up from then on

Groups, plans and Briefs

Drag saves into groups, or keep the ones Nextmark suggests. Turn a group into a plan with a date, and with Pro a Brief sums it up: an overview, key points, open questions and next steps.

  • Your own groups are always unlimited
  • Next Actions collects every step across items and groups
  • Mark items and groups done when you've acted on them
